# yasl
**Y**et **A**nother **S**tack **L**anguage

yasl is a simple stack language.
Its primary purpose is to provide an IR for a future compiler project.
It is also designed to be human-readable and compatible with systems with a wide range of resources.
Detailed information is available in `SPEC.md`.

## Roadmap
Initial execution targets are the 6502 and x86 architectures.
Support for RISC-V and Z80 architectures may be forthcoming.
